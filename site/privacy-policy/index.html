1. About this policy

This policy describes information handling on the {{BRAND}} website (the “Site”). The Site provides general information about final expense insurance and a telephone number for insurance inquiries.

This notice covers the Site’s pages and features. It does not replace the privacy notices of an insurer, agency, call recipient, telephone provider, or other service you choose to use.

2. Website information

Information you enter

The Site does not provide account registration, insurance application forms, payment forms, or fields for entering personal information. You do not need to submit your name, email address, medical information, or payment details to read these pages.

Technical information

The hosting and security services used to deliver the Site may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested page, and request time, to deliver and protect the service. If a hosting platform asks you to sign in before displaying the Site, that provider handles your sign-in information under its own privacy notice.

Analytics and advertising technology

The Site has no added analytics tags, advertising pixels, or behavioral advertising scripts. It does not maintain its own visitor profile database. Hosting services and third-party services you choose to use may handle information under their own policies.

3. Telephone inquiries

When you select a call link for {{PHONE}}, your device opens its calling app or telephone service. If you place a call, your telephone provider and the organization receiving the call handle call information, which may include your telephone number and the time and duration of the call.

Information you choose to provide during a call is handled under the receiving organization’s practices. Ask the representative to identify the organization and provide its privacy notice before sharing sensitive information. Ask that organization about its call recording, retention, and sharing policies.

The Site’s operator may receive compensation for qualifying calls. That advertising relationship does not make this website an insurer or establish insurance coverage.

4. The YouTube video

The embedded video player loads only when you choose to play it. Playing the video connects your browser to YouTube, a Google service. The Site uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed mode.

Google may process network, device, and interaction information, including your IP address and viewing activity, under its Privacy Policy. Privacy-enhanced mode does not mean that Google processes no information. Google may also use cookies or similar storage technologies when its services are used.

You can use the rest of the Site without playing the video. Selecting “Watch on YouTube” opens the video on YouTube, where that service’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

5. Your choices

  • Browse the Site without placing a call or playing the video.
  • Use your browser’s settings to control or clear cookies and other storage.
  • Close the embedded player to stop the current playback and remove it from the page. Closing it does not delete information already handled by YouTube.
  • Ask the organization you contact by phone how to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of information you provide, where those choices are available.

The Site is intended for adults exploring insurance information and is not directed to children.

6. Service providers and external links

Hosting, security, telephone, and video services support different parts of your experience. Each may process information needed to provide its service. Their privacy notices explain their own retention periods, disclosures, security measures, and available choices.

Links to external resources, including insurance information and YouTube, take you to services that have their own terms and privacy practices. No website or communications service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Updates and privacy questions

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. Read this notice together with the privacy information provided by any organization you contact.

For a privacy request about information shared during a telephone inquiry, ask the organization that received your information for its designated privacy contact. The insurance inquiry number displayed on this Site is not represented as a dedicated privacy-request line.
