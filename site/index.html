A few things to know as you explore final expense insurance.

Still have questions? Call us
What is final expense insurance?+

Final expense insurance is life insurance intended to help with funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses. Benefits are generally paid to a beneficiary you choose. The policy determines the amount, conditions, and timing of those benefits.

What can the benefit help pay for?+

A beneficiary can generally use life insurance benefits for funeral or cremation costs, remaining medical bills, and other expenses. It is not limited to paying a funeral home. Review the specific policy and any beneficiary arrangements before purchasing.

How much coverage should I consider?+

Think about the expenses you would like to help cover, any savings or insurance you already have, and a premium you can comfortably maintain. Ask for a clear explanation of the benefit amount and the total ongoing cost before deciding.

Will the full benefit be available right away?+

That depends on the policy. Some policies have a waiting period, a reduced or graded benefit during an initial period, or other exclusions. Ask when coverage begins, when the full benefit becomes payable, and what happens if a claim is made earlier.

Is this the same as a prepaid funeral plan?+

No. Standard final expense life insurance generally pays a beneficiary. A prepaid or pre-need funeral arrangement is typically connected to a funeral provider and specific services. Ask which type of product is being offered and how benefits are paid.

What should I ask when I call?+

Ask who you are speaking with, which insurer issues the policy, the premium and whether it can change, the coverage amount, eligibility requirements, and any waiting periods or exclusions. Request the policy details in writing so you can review them before applying.
